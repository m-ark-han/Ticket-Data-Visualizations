Five more Pinellas ticket figures

These answer five questions from Ranbir's guide using the same 18,278 public Pinellas County pickup tickets as the main gallery. Values are recorded cubic yards (CY). All dates use Eastern time. Hover or tap for exact values, drag to zoom, and double-click a chart to reset.

Source: Pinellas County eGIS Debris Ticket Locations, extracted October 12, 2026. These figures describe recorded tickets, not total debris generated or verified cleanup completion. Loading figures…

Q004 · How much of recorded CY came from the largest days?

Dates with at least one ticket are ranked by CY. Bars show each day's recorded CY; the line shows the cumulative share contributed by the top-ranked dates.

Ranks compare recorded ticket volume. A high-volume date is not proof that more debris was generated that day.

Q009 · Did weekly CY change because of ticket counts, load size, or both?

Three aligned panels compare total recorded CY, ticket count, and mean CY per ticket in each Monday-start week. Total CY equals ticket count multiplied by mean CY per ticket.

This is an arithmetic explanation of the recorded change, not evidence about weather, staffing, routes, or all available trucks. First and last weeks cover only the dates present in the extract.

Q026 · How concentrated is recorded CY among location labels?

Each location is one uppercase, whitespace-normalized CollAddress string. Address labels are ranked by their combined recorded CY. The curve shows the cumulative share from the top-ranked labels; the diagonal is an equal-share reference.

Address names are withheld from this public figure. A label can cover a broad road, park, or staging address and need not represent one property. Blank addresses are excluded from this calculation.

Q053 · Which debris zones rank differently by tickets and CY?

For each of the 30 Zone1 debris-zone labels, the connected dots compare its rank by ticket count with its rank by recorded CY. Rank 1 is highest. Hover or tap a dot for the underlying count and CY.

A rank difference can reflect different recorded CY per ticket. It does not establish which zone had more debris remaining or was served better.
