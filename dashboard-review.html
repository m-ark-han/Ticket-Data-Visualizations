Disaster dashboard and visualization review

Search terms used: disaster dashboard, disaster visualization, hurricane debris collection dashboard Louisiana, Hurricane Laura dashboard, Hurricane Beryl debris dashboard Texas, Post-Beryl Assessment map, and temporary debris sites map. The ten links below were checked on November 16, 2026. Eight are interactive dashboards or maps; the Louisiana site inventory is a data table and La Porte supplies a static zone map. The table and static map are useful references but should not be described as live dashboards. Some interactive examples show disaster impacts or response operations rather than ticket-level collection; their role is labeled explicitly.

Why the PDFs are gone: The earlier PDFs preserved screenshots and historical numbers from dashboards, but they were not dashboards a teammate could explore. The broken Baton Rouge, New Orleans, and Houston links are also removed. This list links to accessible visuals and names the specific design or data lesson taken from each.
