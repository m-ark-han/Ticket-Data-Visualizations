SOURCE & METHODS

What these visuals can—and cannot—show.

The figures use a public Pinellas County eGIS ticket layer associated with the county's hurricane debris dashboard. We grouped the layer's 18,278 rows by the ticket's LoadDate after converting dates to Eastern time.

Ticket totals describe recorded loads. They do not measure all debris generated, material still on the ground, or unique properties served. The county has not confirmed here whether every record is an original pickup rather than another ticket stage.

Locations

Privacy and scope

The interactive subset omits addresses, ticket numbers, and truck identifiers. It contains date, type, volume, incident ID, and coordinates needed for exploration.
