Follow-up review

Low weeks: Among 26 complete weeks, the weeks beginning February 24, March 3, and March 24, 2025 have no ticket records. March 10 has 2 tickets and 38.4 CY. Pinellas County says its final unincorporated-area collection pass ended in mid-January 2025. That supports a wind-down explanation for late low weeks; weather and reporting gaps remain unverified.

Repeated addresses: 93 normalized address labels have at least 10 tickets, accounting for 2,839 tickets and 115,354.2 CY. The largest label has 351 tickets at 349 distinct GPS strings. Park and broad road labels appear among the highest counts. Repetition alone does not establish a duplicate pickup or temporary debris site.

Areas and destinations: F21 compares recorded activity across 30 ticket debris zones. F22 and F23 use the ticket's DisposalSite label to summarize CY by stated destination and zone–destination pair. No figure establishes final disposal or verified cleanup completion.
