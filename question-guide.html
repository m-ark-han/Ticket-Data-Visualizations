Question guide

This guide uses the Q001–Q070 IDs from Ranbir’s question workbook. It maps 11 questions to figures already in the repository and lists the 39 additional figures needed. Some linked figures are archived PNGs outside the nine-figure gallery. A proposed figure is a work item, not a result.

Answered by existing figures (11)

  • Q001 — Daily and weekly recorded pickup CY. F04 daily volume and F05 weekly volume show the recorded CY trend by local pickup date and Monday-start week.
  • Q006 — Dates or stretches with no recorded pickups. F20 ticket-date calendar and F05 weekly volume identify zero-record dates and weeks. A gap is not proof that collection stopped.
  • Q007 — Typical CY per ticket and its distribution. F08 load-volume histogram shows the spread of recorded ticket loads.
  • Q011 — Recorded pickup CY by debris type. F10 type totals and F11 type shares show which labels account for the most CY and tickets.
  • Q013 — Weekly changes in debris-type mix. F12 weekly type CY and F13 weekly type shares show absolute and percentage changes.
  • Q014 — CY per ticket across debris types. F09 load volume by type compares recorded load-size distributions for types with at least 20 tickets.
  • Q016 — Concentrations of recorded pickup CY. F16 interactive volume map aggregates CY by map cell; F15 count map is a ticket-count comparison.
  • Q017 — How the recorded footprint changes over time. F17 monthly footprint compares mapped ticket activity month by month. It describes observed activity, not verified cleanup completion.
  • Q021 — Time between successive recorded pickup dates at one location. F19 revisit intervals uses distinct dates under normalized address labels.
  • Q030 — Repeated addresses with unusually spread pickup coordinates. F26 interactive coordinate-spread map flags address labels for review. Spread alone does not prove an address error.
  • Q033 — Invalid or nonpositive recorded volume values. F02 quality flags checks missing or nonpositive CY; none were flagged in this extract.

New figures to make (39)

Use the full ticket CSV for these figures. Keep date groups in Eastern time, state the location key or grid used for spatial comparisons, and label outputs as recorded ticket activity. Existing figures named below provide starting data but do not fully answer the question.

  1. Q002 — Equal-period volume comparison. Make a bar chart comparing CY in two equally long recent windows, with dates and percent change shown. Use LoadDate and Volume; F04/F05 provide context.
  2. Q003 — Cumulative-volume milestones. Add exact 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90% date markers and a small date table to the cumulative CY curve from F06.
  3. Q004 — Dependence on large days. Rank local pickup dates by CY and plot each day’s contribution plus the cumulative share of total CY.
  4. Q005 — Activity by weekday. Make weekday bars for ticket count and CY; show active dates so weekdays with different date coverage are comparable.
  5. Q008 — CY per active collection date. Plot CY divided by dates with at least one ticket for each week or month, alongside CY per calendar day.
  6. Q009 — Why weekly CY changed. Chart weekly ticket count and mean CY per ticket beside total CY, or decompose the difference between two selected weeks.
  7. Q010 — Rounded/repeated volumes. Plot the frequency of exact Volume values and common rounding increments such as whole, half, and five-CY values.
  8. Q012 — Start and end by debris type. Make a timeline with each raw DebrisType label’s first and last recorded LoadDate and ticket count.
  9. Q015 — Multiple types at one location on one date. Map or list normalized location/date groups with at least two debris types, including type labels, tickets, and CY.
  10. Q018 — First-observed location order. Color a fixed grid or reviewed location key by its first recorded pickup date; make the location definition explicit.
  11. Q019 — Oldest last-observed pickup. Map each location’s most recent recorded LoadDate, or days since it, within the extract’s fixed end date.
  12. Q020 — Distinct pickup dates per location. Map or histogram the number of distinct local LoadDate values at each location; F18 counts tickets instead.
  13. Q022 — First versus later dates. Make stacked bars of CY on the first observed pickup date versus later dates at each location or location group.
  14. Q023 — Debris-type transitions. Make a transition matrix for the type on one recorded pickup date versus the next at a reviewed location key.
  15. Q024 — Repeated same type across dates. Map or rank location/type pairs with that debris type appearing on two or more distinct dates.
  16. Q025 — Duration at each location. Make a location-level map and span distribution of days from first to last recorded pickup. F21 measures an area-level span instead.
  17. Q026 — CY concentration among locations. Rank locations by total CY and plot the cumulative share contributed by the top locations.
  18. Q027 — Moving pickup center. Plot each week’s volume-weighted mean latitude/longitude as dated points on a map, with weekly CY noted.
  19. Q028 — Nearest recorded pickup. Build an interactive point-and-date query that maps and lists nearest ticket coordinates with distance, date, type, and CY.
  20. Q029 — One large day versus many smaller days. Plot each location’s peak-day CY share against its number of active dates and total CY.
  21. Q031 — Coordinates reused across addresses. Rank exact or consistently rounded coordinate pairs by number of distinct normalized addresses and show candidates on a map.
  22. Q032 — Missing fields and affected CY. For each essential field, chart missing ticket count and sum of affected recorded CY; F01/F25 show only part of this.
  23. Q034 — Date-quality exceptions. List and chart unreadable, ambiguous, or outside-window LoadDate values using a stated analysis window; F02 covers basic parse checks.
  24. Q035 — GPS precision and coordinate checks. Chart invalid ranges, missing coordinates, decimal precision, and disagreement between GPS text and numeric Lat/Lng. Do not call a coordinate wrong without an authoritative reference.
  25. Q036 — Debris-type label review. Show raw DebrisType label frequencies and CY beside a proposed category crosswalk. Obtain approval before treating labels as one material category.
  26. Q037 — Ticket ID consistency. Audit blank or repeated TicketId values and, for repeated IDs, compare date, address, type, and CY in a review table.
  27. Q038 — Exact duplicate candidates. Count and list rows identical across all supplied ticket fields, keeping the original row or TicketId for review.
  28. Q039 — Near-match candidates. Make a review table of records close in date, location, type, and CY, with a score or matched-field count. Do not label them confirmed duplicates.
  29. Q040 — QA effect on totals. Make a raw-to-reviewed CY reconciliation chart, showing the contribution of each exclusion or correction rule.
  30. Q051 — Rate-window sensitivity. Compare CY per calendar day over the last 7, 14, and 28 days ending on the same date.
  31. Q052 — Recent daily variation. Plot daily CY in a selected recent window with mean, spread, and zero-record dates clearly marked.
  32. Q053 — Area count/CY rank differences. Use one fixed area boundary or grid, rank areas by tickets and CY, and plot rank disagreements. F15/F16 are separate maps.
  33. Q054 — Hotspot scale sensitivity. Make small-multiple maps at several grid sizes, using consistent periods and explaining how hotspot ranks change.
  34. Q055 — Repeat-location definition sensitivity. Recalculate repeat rates using normalized address, coordinate, and fixed-grid keys and compare results.
  35. Q056 — Contributors to period change. Use a signed contribution chart for change in CY by debris type and fixed area between two equal date windows.
  36. Q057 — Same-day multi-ticket groups. Map or rank location/date groups with two or more tickets, showing their ticket count and combined CY.
  37. Q058 — Largest tickets per day. Plot the share of each day’s CY from its largest 1, 3, or 5 ticket records.
  38. Q059 — Recent rates by debris type. Make bars of CY per calendar day for each DebrisType in a stated recent window, including zero-record dates.
  39. Q060 — Area volume milestones. For each fixed area, plot dates or elapsed days to 50% and 90% of that area’s observed CY; F21 only covers the 90% analogue.

The remaining 20 workbook questions need evidence outside these pickup tickets, such as remaining-debris inventories, verified movement stages, site capacity, invoices, safety logs, or independent service outcomes.
