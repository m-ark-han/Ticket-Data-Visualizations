Receiving-site label investigation

In the St. Petersburg snapshot, DisposalSite is blank for 3,601 tickets. The blanks are concentrated in special services: all 3,566 other special-service tickets and 35 of 41 household hazardous-waste tickets. The other six hazardous-waste tickets show three labels for 7TH AND 22ND DMS and three for NXGEN ENVIRONMENTAL. Ordinary collection and citizen drop-off have no blank DisposalSite values in this snapshot.

HazardClass and DebrisType explain the activity grouping; TicketDate, LoadDate, and Volume do not identify a receiving site. The extracted analysis fields contain no alternate verified receiving-site value. Report “blank in the DisposalSite column,” not “destination unknown.” Check the full city schema, service-specific fields, unload records, and city definitions before making a stronger claim.

Neighborhood timing and clearance definition
  1. Observed activity: show first and last ticket dates, elapsed days, dates with tickets, longest internal gap, ticket count, and matched-ticket coverage.
  2. 90% of recorded volume: if per-ticket CY and neighborhood matching are validated, show the first date cumulative CY reaches 90% of the snapshot total. Label this “days to 90% of recorded CY.”
  3. Verified clearance: use an agency work-area status, final-pass record, or signed inspection with its status definition and date. A last ticket cannot establish clearance.
  4. Estimate-based progress: reconcile units, boundaries, debris types, methods, and timestamps before calculating any completion percentage.

The St. Petersburg figure uses a static October 3–23, 2024 snapshot. Current conditions require a live authoritative status source and refresh time.
