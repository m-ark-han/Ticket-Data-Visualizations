DebrisInsight: ten figures for the dashboard and report

Each figure appears here with its question ID, caption, main takeaway, and data needs. Question IDs come from Ranbir's Pinellas County question list; a related ID does not mean a figure fully answers it. The St. Petersburg and Pinellas County ticket extracts are separate datasets; their totals should not be combined. These are candidates for Sai's dashboard, pending comparison with its existing figures.

The daily activity figure uses the separate St. Petersburg snapshot; all other ticket figures below use Pinellas County records. Do not combine their totals.

1. Daily activity mix — St. Petersburg

Daily collection question · Open the detailed interactive view

Question ID: None from Ranbir's Pinellas County list. This St. Petersburg figure compares ticket counts by activity group, while Q001 asks for recorded pickup volume.

Caption: Stacked daily ticket counts compare ordinary construction-and-demolition or vegetative collection, citizen drop-off, other special services, and household hazardous waste. Dates use local LoadDate.

Takeaway: October 22, 2024 has the most records in the displayed period: 1,179 tickets, including 719 other special-service tickets.

Needs: Local LoadDate, DocumentFormatID, DebrisType, HazardClass, and ticket identifier.

Question ID: Q003 — when recorded volume crossed 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90% of the extract's total.

Caption: The line accumulates each local date's recorded pickup cubic yards (CY). Markers and the table show when the running total first crossed 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90% of this extract's final total.

3. Missing address and GPS-field flags — Pinellas County

F25 · Q032 and Q035 (partial) · Open the interactive street map

Blank collection address GPS text / numeric-coordinate conflict · Pan and zoom; click a point for exact values.

Question IDs: Q032 (partial) — blank collection addresses; Q035 (partial) — disagreement between GPS text and numeric coordinates. Neither full data-quality question is answered here.

Caption: Points flag tickets with no collection address and the one ticket whose GPS text differs from its numeric latitude and longitude. The street-map view lets reviewers inspect locations and exact values.

Takeaway: The map locates 114 tickets with blank collection addresses and one ticket whose GPS text conflicts with its numeric coordinates.

Needs: CollAddress, GPS text, numeric Lat and Lng, and Volume. Authoritative address or geocoding data are needed to validate positions; affected CY and GPS precision require further analysis.

6. Coordinate spread under repeated address labels — Pinellas County

F26 · Q030 · Open the interactive street map

Under 0.25 km 0.25–1 km Over 1 km · Circle size shows ticket count.

Question ID: Q030 — which repeated address labels have unusually spread pickup coordinates.

Caption: Each point represents a repeated collection-address label with at least ten tickets. Its position is the median pickup coordinate; color shows the 90th-percentile distance of that label's ticket points from the median, and point size shows ticket count.

Takeaway: Fifteen repeated address labels have pickup points spread more than 1 kilometer from their median point at the 90th percentile.

Needs: CollAddress, ticket coordinates and IDs, a consistent address-normalization rule, and authoritative address geometry to determine whether a spread is problematic.

7. Zone activity duration — Pinellas County

F21 · M04 and Q025 (partial) · Open the interactive grid

Question IDs: M04 — recorded activity duration by debris zone. Q025 (partial) asks for duration at each pickup location; Q060 (partial) also asks when each area reached 50% of its recorded volume.

Caption: Each zone shows days from its first ticket to the date it reached 90% of its own recorded CY; the detailed view also shows the first-to-last ticket span.

Takeaway: Across 30 debris zones, the time from first ticket to 90% of each zone's recorded CY ranges from 32 to 122 days.

Needs: Zone1, LoadDate, Volume, ticket identifier, and validated zone meanings.

9. Initial and remaining debris estimates — Pinellas County

Original county initial and remaining charts · Proposed paired view

Question ID: None fully answered. Related Q041 and Q048 need dated, comparable estimates and independent evidence of remaining debris.

Caption: The two original dashboard charts show initial and remaining debris estimates. A future aligned view would compare them by district and debris type with estimate date and unit visible.

Takeaway: Vegetative estimates appear across nearly every zone in the initial chart, while visible remaining-estimate bars appear in fewer zones.

Needs: District ID, type, initial estimate, remaining estimate, unit, estimate dates, update cadence, and methods. The captured charts do not show a clear vertical-axis unit.

10. Official area status map — Pinellas County

Original county status map · Completion-status question · Open its source page

Question ID: Q041 (partial) — the county's area-status categories give context, but this captured map does not identify verified remaining debris or all targets still needing collection.

Caption: The original county map colors work areas by the county's recorded pass or status category. A report version should include the status update date.

Takeaway: The dashboard organizes pickup status by labeled zone and distinguishes in-progress, pass-1-complete, and final-pass-in-progress categories.

Needs: Official zone polygons, status code and definition, status date, responsible agency, and source refresh timestamp. A live app should show the current API value and last update.
